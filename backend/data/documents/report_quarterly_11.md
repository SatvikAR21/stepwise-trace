# Q2 2025 Sales Performance Report

**Prepared by:** Revenue Operations, Lumen Home Appliances
**Date:** July 10, 2025

## Summary
Q2 revenue reached $12.4 million, up 8% from Q1 and 15% year over year. Growth was driven by the new SmartCool refrigerator line.

## Key Metrics
- Online sales grew 22% and now represent 41% of revenue.
- Retail partner sales declined 3%, mainly at two regional chains.
- Average order value rose to $612.
- Return rate held steady at 4.1%.

## Findings
1. SmartCool accounted for $3.1 million of Q2 revenue in its first full quarter.
2. The decline in retail partner sales is concentrated in the Midwest.
3. Customer acquisition cost rose 11% due to higher digital advertising prices.

## Recommendations
- Expand SmartCool marketing into Canada in Q3.
- Renegotiate co-marketing terms with the two underperforming retail chains.
- Shift 10% of the paid-search budget to email retention campaigns.

Report owner: Grace Mendoza, Director of Revenue Operations

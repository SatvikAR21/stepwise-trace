SOFTWARE IMPLEMENTATION AND SUPPORT AGREEMENT

This Software Implementation and Support Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into on June 2, 2025 (the "Signing Date") by and between Copperline Systems Inc., a Delaware corporation with offices at 75 Federal Street, Boston, MA 02110 ("Provider"), and Ridgeway Regional Hospital, a non-profit hospital with offices at 400 Hillcrest Avenue, Albany, NY 12208 ("Client").

1. Definitions
1.1 "Platform" means Provider's scheduling and patient-flow software, including all updates Provider makes generally available during the Term.
1.2 "Implementation Services" means the configuration, data migration and training services described in Section 2.
1.3 "Support Services" means the help-desk, maintenance and incident-response services described in Section 5.
1.4 "Client Data" means all data that Client or its staff enter into the Platform, including patient scheduling records.
1.5 "Business Day" means a weekday other than a public holiday in the State of New York.
1.6 "Scheduled Maintenance" means maintenance work announced by Provider at least 48 hours in advance and carried out between 10:00 PM and 4:00 AM Eastern Time.

2. Implementation Services
2.1 Provider shall configure the Platform for Client's three campuses, migrate Client's existing scheduling records, and train up to 120 Client staff members.
2.2 Implementation shall be completed by September 30, 2025, unless the parties agree a new date in writing.
2.3 Client shall provide a project manager, timely access to its systems and complete, accurate data exports.
2.4 Client shall carry out acceptance testing within 10 Business Days after Provider reports that a campus is ready, and shall list any defects in writing.
2.5 If delays caused by Client postpone implementation, the dates in this Section move by the length of the delay, and Provider may charge reasonable additional costs agreed in advance.

3. Fees and Payment
3.1 Client shall pay a one-time implementation fee of $48,000.00, invoiced in two equal parts on the Signing Date and on completion of implementation.
3.2 Client shall pay a subscription and support fee of $12,500.00 per month from October 1, 2025.
3.3 Invoices are payable within 45 days of receipt. Provider may suspend Support Services if an undisputed invoice remains unpaid for more than 60 days after written notice.
3.4 Fees exclude applicable taxes, which Client shall pay where required by law.
3.5 Provider may increase the monthly fee once a year by no more than the change in the US consumer price index, with 60 days' written notice.

4. Term
4.1 This Agreement starts on the Signing Date and continues for 36 months from October 1, 2025 (the "Term").
4.2 The Term renews automatically for successive 12-month periods unless either party gives notice of non-renewal at least 90 days before the end of the current period.

5. Support Services and Service Levels
5.1 Provider shall make the Platform available 99.5% of the time in each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance announced at least 48 hours in advance.
5.2 Provider shall respond to critical incidents within 1 hour and to all other incidents within 1 business day.
5.3 If availability falls below 99.5% in a month, Client shall receive a service credit of 5% of that month's subscription fee for each full percentage point below the target, up to 20% of that fee.
5.4 The help desk is staffed from 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM Eastern Time on Business Days, with an on-call engineer for critical incidents at all other times.
5.5 Provider shall send Client a monthly service report showing availability, incidents and response times.

6. Confidentiality
6.1 Each party shall keep the other party's confidential information secret and use it only to perform this Agreement.
6.2 These obligations survive for five years after this Agreement ends, and indefinitely for patient information.
6.3 Confidential information does not include information that is public, already known to the receiving party, or independently developed without use of the other party's information.

7. Data Protection
7.1 Provider shall process Client Data only on Client's documented instructions and in compliance with applicable health-privacy laws.
7.2 Provider shall notify Client of any security breach affecting Client Data within 24 hours of discovering it.
7.3 On termination, Provider shall return all Client Data in a standard format and then delete it within 30 days.
7.4 Provider may use subprocessors only with Client's prior written consent and remains responsible for their performance.
7.5 Client may audit Provider's compliance with this Section once a year on 30 days' notice.

8. Intellectual Property
8.1 Provider owns the Platform and all improvements to it. Client owns Client Data.
8.2 Provider grants Client a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use the Platform during the Term.

9. Warranties
9.1 Provider warrants that the Platform will perform materially as described in its documentation.
9.2 Provider shall correct any reproducible defect reported by Client at no additional charge.
9.3 Except as stated in this Section, the Platform is provided without any other warranty.

10. Limitation of Liability
10.1 Except for breaches of Sections 6 and 7, each party's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees paid by Client in the 12 months before the claim arose.
10.2 Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential losses.
10.3 Each party shall maintain professional liability and cyber insurance with reputable insurers throughout the Term.

11. Termination
11.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving the other party 90 days' written notice.
11.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other party commits a material breach and fails to cure it within 30 days of being notified.
11.3 On termination, Client shall pay all fees due up to the termination date.

12. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, and the courts of Albany County have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising from it.

13. Notices
Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses above, marked for the attention of each party's legal department. Notices sent by email are valid only if confirmed by letter within five business days.

14. Early Termination Fee
14.1 Notwithstanding Section 11.1, if Client terminates this Agreement for convenience before September 30, 2027, Client shall pay Provider an early termination fee of $95,000.00 within 30 days of the termination date.
14.2 The parties agree that this fee is a genuine estimate of Provider's unrecovered implementation and staffing costs and is not a penalty.

CONSULTING AGREEMENT

This Consulting Agreement is between Meridian Health Partners ("Company") and Alder Strategy Group ("Consultant").

1. ENGAGEMENT. Consultant will assess Company's patient-intake workflow and recommend process improvements.

2. COMPENSATION. Company will pay Consultant $42,000.00 for the engagement: 50% upon signature and 50% upon delivery of the final report.

3. DURATION. The engagement begins upon signature by both parties and ends upon acceptance of the final report by Company.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY. Consultant will not disclose any patient or business information obtained during the engagement.

5. TERMINATION. Either party may terminate with fourteen days' written notice. Fees for work completed up to termination remain payable.

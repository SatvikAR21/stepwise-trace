PILOT EVALUATION REPORT
Automated Invoice Matching at Harbor Logistics Ltd.

Prepared by: Priya Natarajan, Finance Operations Analyst
Date: June 16, 2025

1. Purpose
This report evaluates a twelve-week pilot of LedgerLoop Software's automated invoice-matching tool in the accounts payable team. The pilot ran from March 3, 2025 to May 30, 2025.

2. Method
Two of the five accounts payable clerks used the tool for all supplier invoices, while the other three continued to match invoices by hand. We compared processing time, matching errors and overtime between the two groups.

3. Findings
- Average processing time per invoice fell from 9 minutes to 4 minutes for the pilot group.
- Matching errors dropped from 3.1% to 1.2% of invoices.
- Overtime for the pilot group fell by $18,400 compared with the previous quarter.
- Clerks reported that the tool struggled with handwritten delivery notes.

4. Cost
LedgerLoop did not disclose the licence cost for the pilot. Pricing will only be shared once a full contract is negotiated, so the return on investment cannot yet be calculated.

5. Recommendations
- Request a written price quote from LedgerLoop before any decision.
- Extend the pilot to a third clerk for one more month to test handwritten documents.

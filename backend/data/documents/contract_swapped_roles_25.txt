COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease is made on April 7, 2025 between Westgate Property Holdings LLC ("Landlord") and Brightside Bakery Co. ("Tenant").

1. Premises. Landlord leases to Tenant the ground-floor retail unit at 48 Mill Street, Burlington, VT 05401.
2. Term. The lease runs for three years, starting May 1, 2025.
3. Rent. Tenant shall pay Landlord a monthly rent of $3,400.00, due on the first day of each month.
4. Deposit. Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $6,800.00 before moving in. Landlord shall return the deposit within 30 days after the lease ends, less any unpaid rent or repair costs.
5. Maintenance. Landlord is responsible for the roof and structural repairs; Tenant is responsible for the interior and its baking equipment.
6. Termination. Either party may end the lease with six months' written notice after the first year.

Signed:
Daniel Reyes, Managing Member, Westgate Property Holdings LLC
Amara Okafor, Owner, Brightside Bakery Co.

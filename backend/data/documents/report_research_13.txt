RESEARCH BRIEF: Remote Work and Employee Retention

Prepared for: Human Resources Leadership Team, Crestline Manufacturing
Prepared by: Dr. Amara Nwosu, People Analytics
Published: September 5, 2025

Objective
To determine whether flexible remote-work eligibility is associated with lower voluntary turnover among salaried staff.

Method
We compared 1,240 salaried employees across 14 sites over 18 months, controlling for tenure, role level and salary band.

Findings
- Employees eligible for two or more remote days per week had 27% lower voluntary turnover.
- The effect was strongest for employees with under three years of tenure.
- No measurable difference in performance ratings was found between groups.
- Replacing a salaried employee costs an estimated $38,000 on average.

Recommendations
1. Extend two-day remote eligibility to all salaried roles where on-site presence is not essential.
2. Re-evaluate retention figures after 12 months.

Limitations: observational data; results show association, not causation.

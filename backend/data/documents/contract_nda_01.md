# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into on March 3, 2025 (the "Effective Date") between **Brightline Analytics Inc.**, a Delaware corporation ("Brightline"), and **Harbor & Finch LLP**, a New York limited liability partnership ("Harbor & Finch").

## 1. Purpose
The parties wish to evaluate a potential data-analytics partnership and will exchange confidential information for that purpose only (the "Purpose").

## 2. Obligations
Each party shall (a) keep the other party's Confidential Information strictly confidential, (b) use it solely for the Purpose, and (c) limit access to employees with a need to know.

## 3. Term
This Agreement remains in force for two (2) years from the Effective Date. Confidentiality obligations survive for three (3) years after termination.

## 4. Remedies
Any breach may cause irreparable harm, and the disclosing party may seek injunctive relief in addition to damages.

## 5. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York.

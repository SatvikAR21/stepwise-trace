ANNUAL E-COMMERCE PERFORMANCE REPORT 2025
Fernhill Outdoor Gear Ltd.

Author: Marcus Bell, Head of Digital
Date: January 19, 2026

Overview
Online revenue grew from $2.0 million in 2024 to $2.5 million in 2025. The number of orders rose from 31,000 to 37,200, while the average order value stayed close to $67.

Key findings
- Mobile devices accounted for 61% of all orders, up from 52%.
- Returns fell to 8% of orders after the new size guide launched in May 2025.
- Delivery complaints doubled during the December peak because of carrier delays.

Recommendations
- Add a second delivery carrier before the next December peak.
- Extend the size guide to footwear.

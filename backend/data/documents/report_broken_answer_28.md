## Customer Satisfaction Survey: Q3 2025 Results

Prepared by Lucia Romero, Customer Insights Lead, Vantage Telecom
Published: October 6, 2025

### Summary
We surveyed 4,812 customers between July 1, 2025 and September 30, 2025. Overall satisfaction rose to 78%, up from 74% in Q2.

### Results by channel
| Channel | Responses | Satisfied |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile app | 2,240 | 83% |
| Phone support | 1,515 | 69% |
| Retail stores | 1,057 | 79% |

### Findings
- Phone support remains the weakest channel; the average waiting time was 11 minutes.
- Customers who used the new billing page were more satisfied than those who did not.

### Recommendations
- Add call-back scheduling to phone support.
- Promote the new billing page in the mobile app.

MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

Agreement No. MSA-2025-117

This Master Services Agreement is made on January 15, 2025 between Northwind Logistics Ltd. ("Client") and Cobalt Software Solutions Inc. ("Provider").

1. SERVICES. Provider will maintain and support Client's fleet-tracking platform, including 24/7 incident response and monthly feature releases.

2. FEES. Client shall pay Provider a fixed fee of $18,500.00 per month, invoiced on the first business day of each month and payable within 30 days.

3. SERVICE LEVELS. Provider guarantees 99.9% monthly uptime. For each full percentage point below that target, Client receives a service credit of 5% of the monthly fee.

4. TERM. The initial term runs from February 1, 2025 to January 31, 2026 and renews automatically for successive one-year periods unless either party gives 60 days' written notice.

5. TERMINATION. Either party may terminate for material breach not cured within 30 days of written notice.

Client: Northwind Logistics Ltd., by Marcus Webb, Chief Operating Officer
Provider: Cobalt Software Solutions Inc., by Elena Petrova, VP of Customer Success
